This one sounds boring to a lot of people.

They think their intelligence is fixed. They assume they're either naturally good at something or they're not. They get frustrated when learning feels slow. They mistake repetition for a lack of talent. They quit before their brain has had enough time to adapt.

I'm going to be extremely blunt here. Your brain is not a finished product. It physically changes in response to what you repeatedly do. Every skill you develop, every difficult problem you solve, every uncomfortable thing you practise is an opportunity to rewire your brain.

In a world of AI, your ability to learn, adapt, and think independently is becoming your biggest advantage.

Your brain is trainable.

And as soon as you understand how neuroplasticity works, deliberately practise what you struggle with, and stay consistent long enough for your brain to adapt… your potential changes.

It's a pattern I can't ignore. People who get good at difficult things don't just work harder. They keep exposing their brains to challenges that force them to improve. Stop worshipping talent. Start training your brain. That's the real cheat code. Sorry.

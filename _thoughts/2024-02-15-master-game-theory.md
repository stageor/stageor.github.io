title: "Master game theory"
excerpt: "Intelligence without strategic thinking is wasted potential. Your outcomes depend on what other people do just as much as what you do."
---

This one makes a lot of people uncomfortable.

They think life rewards hard work alone. They assume the best product wins, the most talented person gets promoted, and the most honest person earns the most trust. They ignore incentives. They underestimate competition. They make decisions without thinking about what happens when everyone else makes their own decisions.

I'm going to be extremely blunt here. Intelligence without strategic thinking is wasted potential. You can work harder than everyone else and still lose to someone who understands the game better. In business, relationships, negotiation, and politics, your outcomes depend on what other people do just as much as what you do.

Being smart is not enough.

And as soon as you understand incentives, anticipate other people's moves, and learn to think several steps ahead… you stop reacting to the world and start positioning yourself in it.

It's a pattern I can't ignore. The people who consistently win understand what others want, what they stand to lose, and what will make them change their behaviour. They don't just play the game. They understand the rules, the players, and the payoffs. Master game theory. Stop making moves without thinking about the next move. That's the real cheat code. Sorry.

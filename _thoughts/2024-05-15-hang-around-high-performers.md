---
title: "Hang around high performers"
excerpt: "Your social circle is either raising your standards or lowering them. Your environment is programming you."
---

This one makes a lot of people defensive.

They think they can succeed independently. They believe their habits are entirely their own. They spend years surrounded by people who complain, make excuses, and settle for average. They wonder why their standards never seem to change. They underestimate how much their environment shapes their ambition.

I'm going to be extremely blunt here. Your social circle is either raising your standards or lowering them. The people around you influence what you consider normal, what you believe is possible, and how much effort you're willing to tolerate. In a world where access to information is everywhere, access to the right people can become your biggest advantage.

Your environment is programming you.

And as soon as you start spending time around people who operate at a higher level, observe how they think, and raise your own standards to match theirs… your definition of what's possible begins to change.

It's a pattern I can't ignore. High performers don't just exchange contacts. They exchange ideas, challenge assumptions, and expose each other to opportunities they would never have found alone. They make discipline normal and mediocrity uncomfortable. Stop looking for people who make you feel superior. Find people who make you realise how much you still have to learn. Your circle sets your ceiling. Sorry.

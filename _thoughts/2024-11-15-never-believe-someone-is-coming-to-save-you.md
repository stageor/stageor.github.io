---
title: "Never believe someone is coming to save you"
excerpt: "Nobody is as invested in your future as you are. Your future is your responsibility."
---

This one makes a lot of people uncomfortable.

They think someone will eventually notice their potential. They wait for a mentor to guide them, a manager to give them an opportunity, or a successful person to take them under their wing. They assume hard work will speak for itself. They spend years waiting for permission from people who have no particular reason to give it.

I'm going to be extremely blunt here. Nobody is as invested in your future as you are. People have their own careers, responsibilities, incentives, and problems to solve. Helping you might be a nice thing to do, but it rarely becomes their highest priority. In a world where everyone is competing for attention, expecting someone to rescue you is a terrible strategy.

Your future is your responsibility.

And as soon as you stop waiting to be discovered, start creating your own opportunities, and become valuable enough that helping you makes sense for other people… your circumstances begin to change.

It's a pattern I can't ignore. The people who get ahead don't sit around hoping someone will open the right door. They build relationships, demonstrate competence, ask better questions, and make it easy for others to help them. They learn from mentors without expecting unlimited access to their time. They take responsibility before anyone gives them permission. Stop waiting for someone to see your potential. Give them a reason to take you seriously. That's the real cheat code. Sorry.

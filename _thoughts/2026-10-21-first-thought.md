excerpt: "A short note on why this site has two distinct sections instead of one mixed feed."
---

This section is for opinion, separate from the technical blog. Posts here can be about AI, education, career, or anything else, written as a personal take rather than a tutorial or a write-up.

## How to add a new thought

Create a new markdown file in `_thoughts/` named `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md`, with a title and excerpt in the front matter, then write below it. Same process as a blog post, just in a different folder, so it lands in Thoughts instead of Blog.

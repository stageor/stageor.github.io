I'm an IT Business Analyst and AI Product Owner with over a decade of experience in requirements engineering, systems analysis, product delivery, and digital transformation across enterprise IT and SaaS platforms. I'm currently pursuing an MSc in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at the University of Limerick, Ireland, while working as a Teaching Assistant for the undergraduate Python programming and Scientific Computations module.

Throughout my career, I've worked at the intersection of business, technology, and data, translating complex business requirements into practical technical specifications and helping cross-functional teams deliver software products. My experience includes AI-enabled knowledge management, NLP-driven features, API integrations, data modelling, process automation, and analytics.

Alongside my professional experience, I'm developing my expertise in machine learning and AI engineering through academic study and hands-on projects involving deep learning, natural language processing, classification, and recommender systems. I'm particularly interested in building AI solutions that address real-world problems and deliver measurable value.

I hold a Bachelors from the Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology (MANIT), Bhopal, and am a Professional Member of the International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA). At the University of Limerick, I serve as a class representative for my MSc AI & ML cohort and was selected as one of 54 UL Global Ambassadors.

Based in Limerick, Ireland, I'm interested in opportunities that bring together AI, data, product thinking, and business analysis.
